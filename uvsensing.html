UV Sensing

Why build a UV sensing system?

Melanoma survivors are at increased risk of skin cancer recurrence and must be vigilant about sun protection. However, it is difficult to know when to apply sunscreen and/or protective clothing. Our system aims to help melanoma survivors by providing real-time feedback on UV exposure that is adjusted to account for sun protection, giving actual absorbed dosage and risky exposure alerts.

What can our system do?

Our wrist-worn device measures UV irradience and integrates with a mobile application in which users can input their sun protection (clothing & sunscreen) and skin type (Fitzpatrick). Combining these 3 inputs allows us to calculate an adjusted UV dose continuously, providing within-day risk alerts once absorption levels become risky.
